# Industry topics for the Venture Value Lens

This is the project's mapping of module contexts to SASB industries. Module associations are project choices; they are not SASB classifications of university modules. The disclosure-topic labels were checked against PDF page 3 of each named 2018 standard. All eight standards identify Version 2018-10 on their covers. Full references appear in the [reference list](../REFERENCES.md).

The table contains short topic labels only, arranged for the project. No SASB accounting-metric table, technical protocol or materiality map is reproduced. Preserve source-label spelling, including “Competitive Behavior”, while using British spelling in explanatory prose.

## How to use the list

Read the chosen standard's industry description before selecting 3 to 5 topics. Explain why each topic matters for the venture's activities, affected people and commercial dependencies. The list does not require every topic to be equally important. Record material omissions rather than hiding them. Industry selection and omissions follow the principles in SASB (2018g, pp. 4 to 5) and SASB (2018h, p. 2).

When no industry fits well, choose the closest one, explain where the fit breaks down, and add one venture-specific topic with a justification. Label the addition as a project topic, not a SASB disclosure topic. Note a secondary industry when it contributes a materially different dependency. Use the same exposure, readiness and gap rules for the addition.

Select by the venture's business model, not by the easiest topic list or the student's discipline. These eight mappings are starting contexts. The reusable-container worked example uses Restaurants (FB-RN), an additional standard appropriate to its restaurant-customer context. Its limitations as a proxy are explained in [worked_example.md](worked_example.md).

Topic selection does not establish SASB reporting conformance, ESG performance or regulatory compliance. The resulting map supports the existing Ethical Positioning, Stakeholder Reasoning and Feasibility Judgement clusters.
