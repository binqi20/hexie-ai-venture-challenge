## Steps 3 to 5. Exposure, readiness and gap check

Rate exposure **Low / Medium / High** from the business model, customers, supply chain and location before crediting controls. State the evidence or assumption. Outsourcing does not by itself reduce exposure. Missing information does not justify Low.

Readiness: **0 None; 1 Stated intent; 2 Designed mechanism (specific, owned, costed); 3 Tested mechanism (pilot or evidence).** Level 3 requires evidence that tests the mechanism, with results and limits. Planned tests and AI-generated assurances are insufficient.
