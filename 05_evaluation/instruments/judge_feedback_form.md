## Administration note

Verify relevant consent separately. Use the [judge calibration guide](../../02_assessment_kit/judge_calibration_guide.md) for scores and agreement; this experience form does not replace scoring sheets. Retain initial independent judgements separately from later discussion and consensus. Do not treat a judge's consent as permission to reproduce non-consenting students' work or interaction records. Keep notes minimal, coded and outside this repository; apply your institution's data-protection and research-ethics requirements. Any optional interview follows the approved 15 to 20 minute discussion with consented notes. No recording or automated transcription is authorised here.

Align this form with your institution's approved participant information sheet, consent form and interview protocol before use.
