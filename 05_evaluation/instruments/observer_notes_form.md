Use coded, consent-permitted material only. Exclude mixed-consent team work unless permitted contributions can be separated without exposing others. AI prompts, outputs and responses to AI feedback require the separate optional interaction-data permission. Do not reproduce or reconstruct them when that permission is absent.

## Administration and limits

Observation concerns the activity and resource design. No covert monitoring, full AI histories, passive telemetry, audio/video recording or automated transcription is authorised by this form. Take only necessary written notes with applicable consent, label paraphrases, and avoid identifiable quotations. Keep completed notes outside the repository in restricted institutional storage. Research non-participation does not affect activity eligibility, module marks, academic standing or relationships with the University or teaching staff.

Apply your institution's data-protection and research-ethics requirements for separate consent/linkage storage, withdrawal during the study, retention and destruction of identifiable data, and optional future reuse. Findings will be anonymised or aggregated; individual withdrawal is no longer possible after study completion and anonymisation or aggregation.

Align this form with your institution's approved participant information sheet, consent form and interview protocol before use.
