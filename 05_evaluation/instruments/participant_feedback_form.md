## Administration note

Use a random participant code with the linkage held separately. Offer accessible formats or assistance without recording diagnoses. Collect no demographics or module marks. General research consent covers eligible feedback; research use of AI interaction records requires the separate optional permission. Do not request copied prompts or outputs through this form. An optional discussion uses the approved interview protocol and consented notes, lasts about 15 to 20 minutes, and may be declined. Keep all research-only tasks within approximately 60 minutes; the 90-minute simulation and oral defence are learning activity time.

Keep completed forms outside this repository in restricted institutional storage. Follow your institution's data-protection and research-ethics requirements, including withdrawal, retention and separate optional future-use consent. Completion of this form is not permission for future reuse.

Align this form with your institution's approved participant information sheet, consent form and interview protocol before use.
