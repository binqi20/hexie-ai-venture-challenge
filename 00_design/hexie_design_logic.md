# HeXie design logic

## The theory and its limits

This note paraphrases the three papers listed in the references, using printed pages and PDF locators. They neither prescribe the Challenge nor test its educational effects. The mapping is **the project's interpretation**.

**HeXie theme.** Xi, Cao and Xiangli (2010, p. 204) define the theme through the key issues and core tasks requiring attention within a particular period. It directs attention when detailed strategic prediction is difficult. Xi, Zhang and Ge (2012, p. 405) connect the theme to selecting the appropriate configuration of He and Xie for the current challenge. 席酉民、熊畅、刘鹏 (2020, p. 195) distinguish the shorter-term task from the organisation's longer-term mission and vision, taking environment and resources into account. The theme can change as circumstances and tasks evolve (2020, pp. 199 to 200).

**He principle.** He concerns conditions that activate human initiative and self-determination in uncertain settings. The 2010 paper describes an evolutionary mechanism through organisational circumstances and opportunities for members to act (p. 205). The 2012 account emphasises culture, values and supportive conditions where behaviour cannot be fully specified in advance (pp. 404 to 405). The Chinese review similarly connects human agency with responses to highly uncertain problems (2020, p. 195), elaborating trust and working conditions on pp. 204 to 205. He therefore concerns the conditions for human judgement. Assigning a persona to an AI agent does not itself instantiate this principle.

**Xie principle.** Xie concerns rational design and optimisation where regularities permit processes and behaviour paths to be specified. The 2010 paper describes designed paths and process controls (p. 205; comparison on p. 207). The 2012 paper discusses rules and processes for challenges amenable to scientific reasoning and planning, while recognising limits when human behaviour is unpredictable (pp. 403 to 404). The 2020 review emphasises structures, workflows and systems for relatively determinate problems (pp. 195 to 196). Xie does not imply that every entrepreneurial decision has a predetermined correct answer.

**Coupling.** The principles interact dynamically. Xi et al. (2010, pp. 205 to 206) describe co-evolution between induced initiative and rational design. Xi et al. (2012, pp. 405 to 406) locate their relationship under the current theme. The Chinese review defines coupling as adjustment and fit between the mechanisms while addressing the theme, with successive themes requiring further iteration (2020, p. 196; pp. 199 to 200). Coupling requires more than labelling resources.

## Project interpretation: assessment design

For this Challenge, the proposed HeXie theme is making entrepreneurial judgement visible when AI can assist the production of venture materials. This educational translation extends concepts developed for management. It leaves students responsible for substantive choices while specifying the evidence through which those choices can be examined.

| Challenge element | Project interpretation | Intended mechanism and source boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Staged phases | Xie | Phase A/B/C defines milestones and artefacts. The papers support designed processes generally; they do not prescribe these phases. |
| Capability rubric | Xie | Shared descriptors specify what assessors examine. The five clusters, weights and validity claims cannot be derived from the HeXie papers. |
| AI-use boundaries | Xie | Declarations and verification rules allocate responsibilities. None of the papers supplies generative-AI assessment rules. |
| Decision memo | Coupling | A common structure makes students articulate their own judgement, alternatives and uncertainty. The memo format is a project design choice. |
| Oral defence | Coupling | Structured questions allow students to explain and reconsider decisions. The sources do not test this assessment method. |
| Moderated simulation | Coupling | Shared facts, bounded roles and human moderation structure the encounter; students retain discretion over their response. He applies to human agency. |
| Conflicting feedback | He within Xie safeguards | Divergent priorities require independent judgement; factual controls keep disagreement intelligible. The 2010 discussion permits diversity within order (pp. 201 to 203), without prescribing simulated feedback. |
| Venture Value Lens | Coupling | He informs attention to alignment and tensions among stakeholder interests; Xie informs designed mechanisms and readiness requirements. ESG topics, exposure categories and readiness levels come from the separately documented project method, not these papers. |

Coupling becomes inspectable through the artefacts: teams state an initial position, encounter disagreement, record decisions, revise mechanisms and defend their recommendation. A justified rejection or deferral can be evidence of agency. The same structure constrains unsupported claims without prescribing commercial success or agreement with every stakeholder.

The 2020 review warns that applications can remain preliminary, lack empirical verification, overlook context or attach concepts superficially to practice; it also identifies insufficient work on coupling (p. 207). Accordingly, this mapping supplies a design rationale. Whether the interaction supports useful judgement or learning requires live evidence from the December run.
