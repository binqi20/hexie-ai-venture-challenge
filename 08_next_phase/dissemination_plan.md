# Dissemination plan

Version 1.0, October 2026

## Purpose

No presentation has taken place yet. We plan to present at the Learning and Teaching Colloquium in spring 2027, or an equivalent EDU-led session, after the December run, so that the session can share both the resource package and first evidence from students.

The target is planned; no invitation, event slot or date has been confirmed. The session should help module leaders, facilitators, assessors and educational developers judge whether to adopt the activity, which adaptations are needed and what evidence still limits confidence.

The XJTLU EASE Fund Guidelines (pp. 9 to 11) identify institutional dissemination, reusable resources and good-practice guidance as expected outputs, and the Fund's practical guidance also identifies sharing and Phase 2 scaling. These sources support the planned outputs; they do not establish completion of a presentation.

## Preparation sequence

Before the December run, prepare the story, editable demonstration materials and explicit evidence boundaries. After the run, use the January 2027 evaluation addendum to update `07_adoption_kit/good_practice_note.md` and the presentation. Include student or judge findings only after consent filtering, analysis and disclosure review. If data are incomplete, report what is missing rather than substituting SYNTHETIC results.

Before the target event, confirm the session format with EDU, complete `07_adoption_kit/learning_mall_readiness_checklist.md`, and test the distribution links. Use a SYNTHETIC example for any demonstration. Record actual presentation and publication evidence afterwards, with the event date, materials used and access checks. Keep a presentation record separate from any participant evaluation records.

## Proposed 60-minute staff workshop

The times below sum to 60 minutes. This staff development session is distinct from the 90-minute student simulation, the December human judge calibration protocol and any research time estimate.

| Elapsed time | Activity | Concrete participant work/output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 minutes | Explain the problem, five capabilities and evidence limits | Identify one local assessment decision whose reasoning is currently difficult to see |
| 10 to 20 minutes | Walk through the modular kit and Phase A/B/C submissions | Locate the brief, rubric, declarations and response-log template; choose an adoption route |
| 20 to 35 minutes | Facilitate a SYNTHETIC stakeholder example, using reviewed text or the offline demo | Compare two conflicting role priorities; complete a decision row with a reason and evidence condition |
| 35 to 45 minutes | Practise evidence-led rubric interpretation and AI declaration review | Independently consider a selected cluster, compare evidence pointers, and identify what remains unknown; this brief exercise does not replace full judge calibration |
| 45 to 55 minutes | Adapt the example to a discipline | Complete the adaptation record: local context, industry, persona emphasis, preserved core and one access or workload issue |
| 55 to 60 minutes | Discuss next steps and support | Identify a possible trial owner and one readiness question; record interest only through an agreed route |

Provide accessible materials in advance, avoid compulsory live AI accounts, and have reviewed role cards ready if the interface fails. Any workshop evaluation would need its own confirmed scope and permissions; the December plan does not automatically authorise new research collection.

Participants should leave with `07_adoption_kit/quick_start_guide.md`, `02_assessment_kit/capability_rubric.md`, `02_assessment_kit/ai_use_declaration.md`, `02_assessment_kit/stakeholder_response_log_template.md`, and the adaptation record in `07_adoption_kit/adaptation_guide.md`. Use the Venture Brief heading and fields in the quick start for an editable local brief. Share the project contact, not an invented support appointment.
