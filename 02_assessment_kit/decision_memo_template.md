## 4. Decision Memo

Phase C. Maximum 500 words and one page when completed. Replace prompts and remove guidance; retain the heading. Use the seven prompts as concise paragraphs. Refer to existing evidence by pack section rather than duplicating tables. The suggested allocations total 500 words, including labels.

**Opportunity (about 50 words):** [State the specific opportunity and how its framing changed from Phase A.]

**Key evidence (about 80 words):** [Identify the strongest supporting and contrary evidence. Separate observations, tested claims, assumptions, and simulated stakeholder suggestions. Explain source limitations.]

**Feasibility judgement (about 90 words):** [Choose proceed / pivot / pause / stop. Explain the decisive constraints, alternatives considered, and assumptions across relevant market, technical, regulatory, and resource dimensions.]

**Main stakeholder conflict and resolution (about 85 words):** [Identify the incompatible demands, who was prioritised and why, and the decision recorded in §3.2. Explain any unresolved dependency or deferred condition.]

**Ethical trade-off (about 80 words):** [Identify who might be harmed or excluded, compare an alternative, and defend the chosen position. Reference a concrete mechanism, its owner and cost, and any readiness gap in §5.]

**Final recommendation (about 55 words):** [State the next action, who is responsible, the resource implication, and the decision trigger. Keep this consistent with the feasibility judgement.]

**Remaining uncertainty (about 60 words):** [Name what remains unknown, what evidence would change the recommendation, and how it could be obtained. Do not imply a proposed test has happened.]

**Before submitting:** Check that §§3.2, 4, and 5 agree; make uncertainty explicit; use no personal or confidential information. One-page fit must be checked after completing and exporting the memo.
