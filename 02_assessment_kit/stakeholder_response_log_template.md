Use exactly one of these labels for each decision:

- **ADOPT:** accept the point and state the change it requires.
- **PARTIALLY ADOPT:** identify what is accepted, what is excluded, and why.
- **REJECT:** explain why the point is unsupported, unsuitable, or outweighed by another consideration.
- **DEFER:** identify the missing evidence or unresolved dependency, and the condition for revisiting it.

Every decision needs both a reason and evidence that would change it. Link reasons to an assumption, source, constraint, affected party, or explicit value judgement. Give a testable evidence condition rather than “more research”. When feedback conflicts, explain the priority or practical resolution and cross-reference the other row.
